The platform

Three pinned mechanics. One continuous scroll.

Scroll through the next three chapters. Each one is a live, production-grade module — the same code you'd export from the Studio.

Module 01 · Pinned text & dynamic media

Copy holds still. The world moves behind it.

Pinned text blocks stay anchored to the viewport while hosted images and video cross-fade underneath — driven purely by scroll distance, never by a timer.

Module 02 · Data visualization & charts

Charts that build themselves as you read.

Bars rise from the baseline once their trigger enters the viewport, and map nodes connect in sequence — the geography tells the story before a label is read.

Module 03 · 3D rotation & explosion

A scene bound to the scrollbar.

Feed scroll progress straight into a WebGL render loop. Rotation, tilt and camera dolly all read from a single normalised value, so the narrative resolves cleanly.
